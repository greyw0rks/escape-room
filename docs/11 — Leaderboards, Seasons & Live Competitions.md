# AI Escape Room

## Document 11 — Leaderboards, Seasons & Live Competitions

Version 1.0

---

# Executive Summary

Leaderboards are not simply rankings.

They are the primary reason players return every day.

The objective of the competitive system is to transform AI Escape Room from a puzzle game into a living competition where thousands of players attempt the same room, compare strategies, improve their skills, and earn recognition.

Winning should feel prestigious.

Participation should always feel worthwhile.

---

# Core Philosophy

Players compete against intelligence.

Not against character levels.

Not against expensive NFTs.

Not against purchased upgrades.

Every player begins each room under identical conditions.

Only skill determines the outcome.

# Global Leaderboard

Every ranked room contributes to the global leaderboard.

Players are ranked by:

* Escape Rating
* Tournament Points
* Seasonal Score
* Win Percentage
* Puzzle Accuracy

The leaderboard should update in near real time.

---

# Daily Challenge

Every 24 hours:

One room.

One puzzle seed.

One leaderboard.

Everyone plays exactly the same room.

Rules:

* Single official attempt.
* Same puzzle for everyone.
* Same timer.
* Same scoring algorithm.

Daily Challenges become the heartbeat of the game.

---

# Daily Rewards

Possible rewards:

Top 1%

Large crypto reward.

Top 10%

Medium reward.

Top 25%

Small reward.

Participation reward (optional)

XP, badge progress, cosmetics.

This encourages players of every skill level to participate.

# Matchmaking

Competitive rooms should group players with similar Escape Ratings when appropriate.

Benefits:

Fairer competition.

Better retention.

Reduced frustration for beginners.

Global tournaments remain open to everyone.

---

# Ranking Algorithm

Ranking should consider:

Escape time.

Puzzle accuracy.

Hint usage.

Puzzle efficiency.

Secret discoveries.

Completion consistency.

The fastest player should not automatically be the winner if accuracy is poor.

---

# Tie Breakers

Priority order:

Higher score.

Fewer hints.

Shorter completion time.

Earlier completion timestamp.

Deterministic tie-breaking prevents disputes.

---

# Ghost Runs

Top players' escape paths may be replayed.

Players can watch:

Movement.

Object interactions.

Puzzle order.

Decision timing.

This helps others improve without revealing hidden AI prompts.

Ghost replays become valuable learning tools.

# Acceptance Criteria

The Competitive System is complete when:

* Daily Challenges refresh automatically.
* Leaderboards update accurately.
* Seasons reset cleanly.
* Rankings prioritize skill over speed alone.
* Players can compare performance globally and with friends.
* Competitive events remain fair and transparent.
* Rewards reinforce mastery rather than participation alone.

---

# Competitive Advantage

Most puzzle games are solitary experiences.

AI Escape Room transforms every puzzle into a shared global event.

Thousands of players may enter the exact same room.

Each will:

Notice different clues.

Take different routes.

Ask different questions.

Finish with different scores.

The room is shared.

The experience is personal.

That combination creates endless discussion, strategy sharing, and friendly rivalry.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The ultimate goal is for players to wake up each morning asking:

> "What's today's room?"

Not because they have to maintain a streak.

But because they genuinely want to test themselves against the rest of the world.

When the Daily Challenge becomes part of a player's routine, AI Escape Room evolves from a game into a daily competitive ritual.

---

# Final Principle

Winning should feel meaningful.

Losing should feel educational.

Every leaderboard should inspire one thought:

> **"I can do better tomorrow."**

That mindset keeps players returning long after they have mastered the mechanics.
