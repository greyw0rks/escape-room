# AI Escape Room

## Document 10 — Crypto Economy, Rewards & Anti-Cheat

Version 1.0

---

# Executive Summary

The Crypto Economy is not a monetization feature.

It is part of the gameplay.

Players enter rooms by risking a small amount of crypto.

Players escape through skill.

Players earn rewards based on performance.

The economy must reward intelligence, fairness, and competition while remaining sustainable over the long term.

The objective is **not** to create another Play-to-Earn game.

The objective is to build a **Play-to-Compete** game.

---

# Core Philosophy

Players should earn because they solved puzzles better than others.

Not because they played longer.

Not because they own expensive NFTs.

Not because they bought upgrades.

Skill creates value.

---

# Design Principles

The economy should be:

Fair.

Transparent.

Skill-based.

Sustainable.

Bot-resistant.

Simple to understand.

Reward eligibility.

# Reward Distribution Models

The engine should support multiple formats.

---

## Leaderboard Model

Top performers receive rewards.

Example:

1st

30%

2nd

20%

3rd

10%

Remaining top finishers split the rest.

---

## Performance Threshold

Players who exceed a score threshold receive rewards.

This model encourages participation rather than only rewarding the fastest players.

---

## Tournament Model

Scheduled competitions.

Fixed prize pools.

High entry fees.

Large rewards.

One attempt.

---

## Sponsored Rooms

Partners may fund prize pools.

Players compete without relying entirely on entry fees.

This creates marketing opportunities without affecting fairness.

# Anti-Cheat Philosophy

Cheating must never be more profitable than playing fairly.

Every economic decision should assume attackers exist.

---

# Cheat Detection

Monitor:

Impossible completion times.

Automated input.

Repeated identical actions.

Prompt injection attempts.

Multiple wallet abuse.

Replay manipulation.

Abnormal interaction patterns.

Flag suspicious sessions for review.

---

# AI Prompt Protection

Players must never access:

Hidden prompts.

Puzzle solutions.

Internal validation logic.

Developer instructions.

The AI should remain fully sandboxed.

---

# One Attempt Integrity

Ranked rooms may enforce:

Single attempt.

Server-side timing.

Immutable submissions.

Replay validation.

This protects leaderboard integrity.

---

# Server Validation

Critical events should always be verified server-side.

Examples:

Room completion.

Score calculation.

Reward eligibility.

Prize distribution.

Never trust the client for financial decisions.

---

# Bot Resistance

Possible mechanisms:

Behavior analysis.

Interaction timing.

Session fingerprinting.

Rate limiting.

CAPTCHA during suspicious activity.

Wallet reputation.

No single defense is sufficient.

Security should be layered.

---

# Fraud Prevention

Prevent:

Duplicate reward claims.

Multiple simultaneous sessions.

Artificial latency manipulation.

Wallet farming.

Prize pool abuse.

All economic actions should leave an auditable trail.

---

# Transparency

Players should always know:

Entry fee.

Prize pool.

Scoring rules.

Reward calculation.

Distribution timing.

Transparency builds trust.

---

# Future Expansion

The economy should support:

Guild tournaments.

Creator revenue sharing.

Community-funded prize pools.

Sponsored championships.

Streaming competitions.

Cross-game rewards.

These remain outside the MVP.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The Crypto Economy is complete when:

* Players can easily enter ranked rooms using crypto.
* Rewards are distributed fairly based on skill.
* Practice Mode remains completely free.
* Cosmetic purchases never affect gameplay.
* Anti-cheat systems protect competitive integrity.
* All financial operations are validated server-side.
* The economy remains understandable to both crypto-native and non-crypto players.

---

# Competitive Advantage

Many Web3 games reward ownership.

Others reward grinding.

AI Escape Room rewards performance.

Every player enters the room under the same conditions.

The only difference is:

Observation.

Reasoning.

Decision making.

Execution.

That makes winning feel earned rather than purchased.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The long-term goal is to create a trusted competitive ecosystem where players are willing to pay a small entry fee because they believe:

* The competition is fair.
* The puzzles are excellent.
* The rewards are transparent.
* Skill consistently determines the outcome.

When that trust exists, the crypto economy becomes an invisible layer supporting an exceptional game rather than the reason people play.

---

# Final Principle

Players should never open AI Escape Room thinking:

> "How much money can I make today?"

They should think:

> "I wonder if I can beat everyone else today."

If they succeed, the crypto rewards become proof of their skill, not the motivation for playing.

That distinction is what separates a lasting competitive game from a short-lived Play-to-Earn experiment.
